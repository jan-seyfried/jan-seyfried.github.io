---
title: "Optimal Sample Complexity Lower Bounds on Conditional Independence Testing"
---
Joint work with Neelkanth Mishra, Sayantan Sen, and Marco Tomamichel<br>
<span style="display:block; height:0.5em;"></span>
We resolve the remaining open questions in the sample complexities of both conditional independence and conditional mutual information testing, up to logarithmic factors. To do this, we generalize the lower bound constructions by [Canonne, Diakonikolas, Kane and Steward (STOC 2018)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3188745.3188756).<br>
